Ambient particles add movement without taking over the full Scene. Start with lower Density and Speed, then increase them only when the effect needs to be more visible.

## Foliage

[Autumn Leaves](../../reference/effects/details/autumnleaves/) and [Summer Leaves](../../reference/effects/details/summerleaves/) support ripple, depth variation, directional travel, and background accumulation. [Summer Leaves](../../reference/effects/details/summerleaves/) also includes a **Variants** control for choosing which leaf and petal designs can appear. [Sakura Bloom](../../reference/effects/details/sakurabloom/) and [Sakura Blossoms](../../reference/effects/details/sakurablossoms/) provide blossom-focused alternatives.

## Light, magic, and spectral effects

[Embers](../../reference/effects/details/embers/), [Stars](../../reference/effects/details/stars/), [Magic Crystals](../../reference/effects/details/magiccrystals/), and [Ghosts](../../reference/effects/details/ghosts/) can be used around light sources, magic areas, or specific parts of a map. [Ghosts](../../reference/effects/details/ghosts/) supports manual placement when it needs to start from exact Scene locations.

## Water ambience

[Bubbles](../../reference/effects/details/bubbles/) provides underwater ambient motion.
