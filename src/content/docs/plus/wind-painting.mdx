---
title: Wind Painting
description: Author a manual Wind mask with the FXMaster+ brush workflow.
---

Wind Painting limits the [Wind](../../reference/effects/details/wind/) filter to areas painted directly on the Scene or inside a Region. The painted mask is saved with that Wind effect.

## Paint a Wind mask

### Enter painting mode

1. Select <span class="fxm-control-label" data-control="filters">Filter Effects</span> from <span class="fxm-control-label" data-control="effects">FXMaster Controls</span>, then add or edit the FXMaster+ [**Wind**](../../reference/effects/details/wind/) filter. For a Region, open **FXMaster: Filter Effects** and edit Wind there.
2. Enable **Manual Painting**.
3. Set **Brush Radius (Grid Spaces)**.
4. Under **Painting**, use **Paint**, **Erase**, or **Clear**.
5. Paint over the Scene or Region while watching the brush and overlay preview.
6. Select **Save** after the mask and Wind options are configured.

### What to paint

Paint over map artwork that should visibly sway or ripple when Wind is active. Common examples include:

- Tree canopies, branches, and leaves.
- Bushes, shrubs, tall grass, and crops.
- Tents, awnings, curtains, and other hanging fabric.
- Flags, banners, pennants, and sails.
- Vines, loose cloth, and other flexible environmental details.

Keep the mask close to the parts of the artwork that should move. Avoid covering rigid structures or the surrounding ground unless that distortion is part of the intended result.

### Brush size

Use a small brush for boundaries and narrow passages. Use a large brush to establish broad coverage, then refine the edges. The Wind effect exposes a normalized brush control in its configuration, while the runtime constrains the final brush size to a safe supported range.

### Clear or revise a mask

Use **Paint** to add coverage, **Erase** to remove coverage, and **Clear** to remove the complete mask for the current Scene or Region. Disabling **Manual Painting** returns the Wind row to full procedural coverage; the saved paint mask is not used while **Manual Painting** is disabled.

## Wind behavior

### Combine with procedural controls

Painting defines where Wind movement is present; the normal Wind options still define its character:

- Direction and direction randomization.
- Speed and density.
- Distortion and turbulence.
- Sway strength and frequency.
- Gustiness, streakiness, sheen, and opacity.

### Synchronized effects

Compatible particles and filters can still synchronize direction with a painted Wind row. The mask limits Wind’s visible field, while the row’s direction state remains available to dependent effects.
