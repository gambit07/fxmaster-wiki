## Custom particle bases

FXMaster+ custom particle definitions inherit their available parameters from one of four bases:

- [Directional Drift](./details/custom-directional-drift/) moves custom images in a selected direction and can use Top Down, Orbit, a persistent background, and token interaction.
- [Ambient Float](./details/custom-ambient-float/) distributes custom images across the visible area without a selected travel direction.
- [Manual Placement](./details/custom-manual-placement/) places custom images at exact Scene positions.
- [Sound Only](./details/custom-sound-only/) creates no visible particles and activates matching SoundFX rules.

Create and manage these definitions through [Custom Particle Effects](../../plus/custom-particles/).
