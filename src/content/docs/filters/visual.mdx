Visual filters change the final appearance of the Scene. Add them after the main particles and environmental filters so it is clear what each visual filter is changing.

- [**Bloom**](../../reference/effects/details/bloom/) amplifies bright areas and glow.
- [**Color**](../../reference/effects/details/color/) applies scene color treatment.
- [**Old Film**](../../reference/effects/details/oldfilm/) adds aged-film artifacts.
- [**Predator**](../../reference/effects/details/predator/) creates a stylized thermal or refractive treatment.
- [**Glitch**](../../reference/effects/details/glitch/) introduces digital disruption.
- [**Sunlight**](../../reference/effects/details/sunlight/) creates directional light treatment.
- [**Lightning**](../../reference/effects/details/lightning/) provides full-scene flash behavior.
- [**Screen Shake**](../../reference/effects/details/screenshake/) moves the view for timed or audio-triggered impact.
